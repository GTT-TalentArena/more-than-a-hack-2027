# Deviations (variant B + Claude)

> 🇪🇸 English translation of [desviaciones.md](desviaciones.md).

- **Variant outside the original design.** The protocol pits A (Opus) against B (GPT); this variant mixes the two providers on purpose. Approved by Alejandro (judge, A1) on 2026-10-21 at 21:33. B's v0 is kept intact in the tag `b-gpt-v0`.
- **Inherited from v0.** v0 already declares in its record that some prompts to Codex were written with Claude's help.
- **Public quota and hours.** Decided by Alejandro on 2026-10-21 (21:45–21:46): this variant **shares** candidate B's 5 public evaluations (4 left) and its hours **count within** B's 8 h.
- **Separation (A10).** Claude has not looked at the `cand/a-opus` branch and does not know candidate A's approach.
- **v3 inspired by the public window.** The v3 rule (a gap without the ball is only a stoppage if it starts or ends like a restart) was thought of **after** seeing the aggregate breakdown of public evaluation #3 (D precision and recall). It is a football rule, not a tuned parameter, and the "player with their back to the camera hiding the ball" case was proposed by Edu; even so, there is a risk of overfitting to the 17 public possessions. Criterion fixed before evaluating it: if it does not clearly improve on v1, the final version of the variant is v1.
- **Effort change (22:18).** For v4, Edu raised Claude Code's effort from **medium** to **max** (same model, Opus 5.5). A switch to Fable 5.1 was attempted, but it was not available. v1–v3 were made with medium effort.
- **Everything local.** At the team's request, nothing from this variant (branches, tag, record) is pushed to GitHub until further notice.
- **v4 decision rules not followed to the letter.** (1) P3 met its criterion (≥ 0.5 min), but it is not applied: the criterion measured minutes, not whether they were false `D`, and the data suggest those gaps are real restarts. (2) P6 met the ratio (4 versus 1 rebound), but with 4 events and ≤ 5 s of effect it does not justify a perspective correction. (3) P4 had no numeric threshold ("many"); it was implemented as a minimum duration for a change of possession (3 s) instead of contact detection. Details in analisis-v4.md ([English](analisis-v4.en.md)), §6.
- **Comparison brought forward (22 Oct, 17:00).** Alejandro compared the candidates on 22 October, not on 30–31 October as planned, and chose **candidate A**. A's PR (#33) was merged into `main` before B was pushed. At the judge's request, B is pushed afterwards to keep a record of the comparison, and B's branch merges `main`. The initial plan was to remove B's code from `main` afterwards, but on 22 October the team agreed to keep both candidates in `main` so they can be run side by side with the shared Docker; the removal PR (#39) was closed without merging.
