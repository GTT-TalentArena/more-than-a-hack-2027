
# GTT — TalentArena

**Turning football data into meaningful insights.**

We are GTT, a five-member team participating in **More Than a Hack 2027**, the football analytics hackathon at Talent Arena in Barcelona.

Our team combines data analysis, algorithm development, evaluation, and engineering to explore how data can help us better understand football.

> **Current focus:** estimating team possession frame by frame from automatic tracking data and pitch homography.

## The Challenge

The initial challenge focuses on estimating which team has possession
of the ball in every frame of a football match, using the tracking
data and homography provided by the organizers.

Our objective is to build a reliable, reproducible, and measurable
solution, validate our assumptions, and improve it through controlled
experiments.
